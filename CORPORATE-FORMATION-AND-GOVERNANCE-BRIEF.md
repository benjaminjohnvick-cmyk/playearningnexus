# Corporate Formation & Governance — Counsel Brief

> **⚠️ NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** An inventor-prepared brief flagging the entity, governance, and ownership work that
> should be completed before launch, fundraising, or any IP filing. Foundational: most other packet items (ToS,
> EULA, contracts, patent/trademark assignments, tax registrations) depend on the entity existing and owning the
> IP. Prepared 2026-09-14. See master list `LAWYER-MASTER-TODO` §0 and Part B.

## Why this matters

Every `[LICENSOR]` blank in the contract stack, the patent and trademark assignments, the tax registrations, and
the bank/payment-processor onboarding all require a formed legal entity that clearly owns the platform and its IP.
Operating without it exposes the owner personally and clouds title to the IP right before it is most valuable.

## What counsel should set up

- **Entity choice & formation** — LLC vs. S-corp vs. C-corp (weigh liability, tax pass-through, and any future
  outside-investment plans with the CPA); file articles; obtain the **EIN**; appoint a **registered agent**.
- **Governing documents** — operating agreement (LLC) or bylaws + organizational resolutions (corp), including
  ownership/units, management authority, and signing authority for the contracts in this packet.
- **IP assignment into the entity** — signed assignment of ALL pre-formation IP (source code, brand, domain,
  content, and inventions in the provisional) from the founder(s) into the entity, dated before any USPTO filing.
- **Founder / contractor invention-assignment & confidentiality (PIIA)** — so anything built by anyone flows to
  the entity; ties to the Partner/Developer Agreement and the NDA templates brief.
- **FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI)** filing; state/city business licenses and any seller's permit.
- **Corporate housekeeping** — separate bank account, books, and minute book; avoid commingling (preserves the
  liability shield the entity is for).

## Decisions for counsel/owner

Entity type and home state; who holds signing authority; whether any equity is promised to advisors/contractors
(affects the assignment and securities posture); timing of the IP assignment relative to the patent/trademark
filings in the IP folder.
