## Our community in one line

Get Goods Gratis is an 18+ community for earning and shopping. Be honest, be kind, keep it legal — and everyone
gets to keep earning.

## The rules

- **18+ only.** You must be 18 or older. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed.
- **Be real.** No fake accounts, bots, self-dealing, or gaming the earn/referral system. Rewards from fraud are
  reversed.
- **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate, threats, or targeting other members — including in Buddy Chat and any
  voice or messaging feature.
- **Keep it lawful and safe.** No illegal content or activity. **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is reported
  to NCMEC and law enforcement**, with zero tolerance.
- **Post only what's yours.** Don't upload content you don't own or have a license to; respect others' IP
  (see the DMCA process).
- **Disclose paid posts.** If you earn from sharing an offer, your post must clearly say so (e.g. `#ad`).
- **Protect privacy.** Don't share other members' personal information.

## How enforcement works

Violations can lead to content removal, feature limits, reward clawback, suspension, or account termination,
depending on severity — with an appeals path. The binding terms are in the Acceptable Use Policy and Terms of
Service.
