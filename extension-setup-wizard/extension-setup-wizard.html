Load the browser extension, flip its gates, publish it, and verify — step by step.

The extension (gg-extension, Manifest V3) is code-complete. It earns members non-cashable Site Cash on its own new-tab/popup surfaces and offers clean-attribution cashback. Everything is gated OFF server-side until you turn it on. This wizard walks the go-live: load & test, flip the server flags, publish to the Chrome Web Store, and verify.

The extension lives in the repo at browser-extension/gg-extension and in Bundle 38 under 09 - Browser Extension. A store-ready gg-extension.zip is built alongside it.

2 · Flip the server gates

counsel-gatedRailway → Variables → Raw Editor

Nothing rewards, tracks, or pays until you turn these on. The master switch plus each layer is separate. Rewards enrollment is opt-out (on) by default; tracking is opt-in.

Two compliance lines first. The affiliate layer needs a connected network account, and the tracking layer (Layer B) is a privacy line — clear both with your lawyer before enabling. Money/points stay closed-loop and non-cashable.
# Extension — Railway raw block
# Master switch — turns the extension backend on
EXTENSION_ENABLED=1
# Own-inventory ads on the extension's own surfaces (new-tab/popup) — the reward layer
EXTENSION_OWN_ADS_ENABLED=1
# Reward economics (points; 100 points = $1 of non-cashable Site Cash)
EXTENSION_REWARD_PER_IMPRESSION_POINTS=5
EXTENSION_REWARD_DAILY_CAP_USD=1
EXTENSION_REWARD_LIFETIME_CAP_USD=200
# --- Counsel-gated layers: leave OFF until cleared ---
# Affiliate cashback (needs a connected network account)
EXTENSION_AFFILIATE_ENABLED=0
EXTENSION_AFFILIATE_USER_SHARE_PCT=0.5
# Layer B browsing/personalization (opt-in; privacy review)
EXTENSION_TRACKING_ENABLED=0
# Chrome Web Store listing URL (set after you publish — step 3)
EXTENSION_WEBSTORE_URL=

Last step — verify

Install from the store (or reload the unpacked build), sign in, open a new tab, tap Watch to earn, wait the 5-second view, and confirm Site Cash credited. Check the daily cap holds (a second view past the cap returns capped, credits 0). Confirm the popup's rewards toggle and the Settings page all read your live state.
