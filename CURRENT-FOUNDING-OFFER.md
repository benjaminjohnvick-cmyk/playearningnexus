# The Founding (Pre-Launch) Advertiser Offer — Current Offer

*A clean, standalone description of the founding pre-launch advertiser offer as it stands today. Get Goods Gratis
(Free) — an 18+ closed-loop retail / e-commerce rewards marketplace. Intended web address: getgoodsgratis.com.*

---

## What it is

The **Founding (Pre-Launch) Advertiser Offer** is a limited, introductory advertising + membership package for
the first businesses to come aboard before public launch. It is the best deal the platform will ever offer, and
founders keep it for life.

## Price and availability

- **$13,000 / year**, billed as **$1,000 every 4 weeks across 13 cycles**, as a **4-year package**.
- **200,000 founding-advertiser slots.** The offer stays open until 200,000 founding advertisers enroll, then it
  closes and becomes the standard Tier 1 offer at **+30% ($16,900/year)**. Founders keep their **founding price
  for life**.
- **Category exclusivity** — each founder is the only founding advertiser in their category.
- **Grandfathered forever** — founders keep the entire founding perk stack permanently, even after Tier 1 takes
  over.

## Free-trial entry — start free, then auto-convert to paid

A business can **start the founding offer free** — no payment required to begin — and it **automatically converts
to the paid founding plan**. The conversion is built to be clean and fair:

- **Recurring billing, not a lump sum** — conversion bills the standard 13-cycle schedule ($1,000 every 4 weeks),
  cancelable after any cycle.
- **You're only charged after you've received value** — the conversion triggers once the audience is in place
  and advertising has begun delivering, not on a bare calendar date.
- **A clear signed agreement** — the conversion terms are set out in a business order form the advertiser signs,
  not buried in fine print.
- **Plain, explicit consent** — a standalone confirmation of exactly what will be charged and when, recorded at
  signup and restated by email.
- **Reminders + one-click cancel** — advance reminders before the first charge, and canceling is as easy as
  signing up.
- **A refund window** — a money-back window after conversion if it wasn't intended.

## The advertising

- **200,000 ad impressions / year** across the between-survey and social surfaces, on the 4-year term, with
  **priority/premier placement** and a spot on the sponsors wall.
- **100,000 launch-bonus impressions** (one-time).
- **Capacity-paced delivery, deliver-until-met** — advertising is guaranteed by **amount, not by date**: it
  delivers as the audience grows, and the platform keeps delivering, free, until the full promised amount is
  received. The guarantee is **advertising delivered**, never revenue, sales, or ROI.
- **Value stack** — $13,000 buys roughly **$26,000 (2×) of advertising value**, backed by guaranteed
  value-match impressions.
- **Audience-growth dividend** — the impression allotment grows automatically as the user base grows.

## Everything included

- **Premium membership.**
- **Always-on AI campaign manager + optimization**, and **priority concierge support** (human escalation
  available).
- **Free AI-written ad creative** with ongoing refresh, and **~30 AI social ad posts / month** (clearly labeled).
- **A/B testing, real-time analytics & attribution, and consumer-sentiment insights.**
- **$2,000 Site Cash grant** — non-cashable store credit, released over the term.
- **Every feature at its maximum (Tier 3 / Unlimited) capability, free** — a founding advertiser is provisioned
  at the highest capability level for every tier-gated feature: maximum AI Creative Suite generations, all ad
  formats, unlimited concurrent experiments, multivariate testing, the full campaign-automation ceiling,
  predictive learning, and image / video / brand-kit / localization / auto-refresh. This is the most scaled-up
  version of every feature, at no extra cost. (It maxes feature **capability**, not the delivered ad-impression
  **volume** above.)
- **Keep 100% of your own survey earnings — for life** (as non-cashable Site Cash), a better share with no cap;
  the standard rate is 75% for post-close members.

## Founder-only perks

- **Founding price locked forever** — the $13,000 rate never rises for as long as they stay.
- **"Founding Partner" verified badge** + a permanent spot on the public Founding Partners wall.
- **Founder referral bonus** — refer another advertiser, earn bonus impressions / account credit.
- **Dedicated onboarding + quarterly strategy sessions.**
- **Roadmap input / founder advisory**, and first beta access to every new ad surface & AI tool.
- **Co-marketing** — launch PR, "founding partners" announcements, case studies.
- **Annual free creative refresh / seasonal campaign packs.**
- **Better make-good / extended cancellation terms** than standard Tier 1.

## The two launch gates

Full delivery is gated on reaching **both**:

1. **200,000 founding advertisers**, and
2. a separate **200,000 regular premium users** — the audience founders advertise to.

Together that's roughly a **400,000-user** audience in place before full public launch.

## How the funds are used (two-phase)

- **Phase 1** — until **both** user milestones are met (the 200,000 prelaunch users **and** the additional
  200,000 regular users, ~400,000 total), the founding funds are **directed to acquiring those users and building
  that audience**.
- **Phase 2** — once Phase 1 is complete, the funds are the **owner's to use at its discretion for any lawful
  business purpose**.

Founding contributions are **non-refundable** (presale model).

## No obligation on the free paths

Alongside the paid founding path there are participation paths — a **no-upfront** path and a **free
earn-to-unlock** path — which are delivery schedules, never debts: stop anytime, owe nothing, no penalty.
