## Description (both stores)
GamerGain is a play-to-earn platform where your time turns into points you can actually use.

Earn points by completing surveys, offers, and daily activities. Points are worth one cent of your
local currency inside the app, so what you see is always in your own money.

Spend points in a Facebook-Marketplace-style store: an original, AI-built product catalog organized
across dozens of departments and thousands of subcategories, plus items listed by other members.
Prefer the real branded product? Tap "find the real thing" to search across major retailers in your
country. Buy with points, or by card where available.

The app automatically speaks your language and shows prices in your currency, and adapts to the country
you're in.

Highlights:
- Earn points from surveys, offers, and daily tasks
- Local currency and language, automatically
- A huge, always-growing product catalog
- Buy with points or card; members can sell their own items with privacy
- Personalized recommendations and a helpful in-app assistant

GamerGain rewards participation and merit — it is not gambling and involves no wagering. You must be 18+
to use the app.
