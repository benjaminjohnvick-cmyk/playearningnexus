# Marketplace Seller Terms

**Effective date: July 28, 2026** · Version 2026-07-28

These Seller Terms apply when you list an item on the GamerGain marketplace (including relisting an item
you own). They supplement, and are part of, the Terms of Service. Not legal advice — have counsel review
before launch.

## 1. Who can sell
You must have an account in good standing and be 18+. By listing, you represent you have the right to
sell the item and that the listing is accurate.

## 2. Your listing
- Provide an honest title, description, condition, and price (in points and/or, where enabled, card).
- Do not misrepresent items, use others' photos or trademarks without rights, or list items you cannot
  deliver.
- You may list **anonymously** (relisting): your name and location are not shown. You remain responsible
  for the sale.

## 3. Prohibited and regulated items
You may not list: weapons, ammunition, or explosives; drugs, controlled substances, or paraphernalia;
alcohol, tobacco, or vaping products; counterfeit, stolen, or infringing goods; recalled or unsafe
items; adult sexual content; live animals; hazardous materials; regulated financial or medical products;
government IDs; or anything illegal in the buyer's or seller's location. We screen listings and remove
violations; repeated violations lead to suspension.

## 4. Fulfillment and shipping
For member listings, **you are responsible for shipping** promptly after a sale and for providing
tracking where available. Orders run through our AI-managed fulfillment process (escrow → you ship →
funds/points release). If you do not ship within the stated window, the order may be cancelled and the
buyer refunded.

## 5. Pricing, points, and payouts
Points received for a sale are credited to your account per the closed-loop rules in the Terms of
Service. Where card sales are enabled, a platform markup applies to the buyer and disclosed fees may
apply to you. Points are not cash and are not withdrawable unless and until cash-out is enabled.

## 6. Cancellations, returns, and disputes
Honor the Refund Policy and any return window shown. If a buyer opens a dispute, respond with evidence
promptly; our process decides based on the evidence and may reverse the sale and return the buyer's
points. Excessive disputes or cancellations may limit or suspend your selling privileges.

## 7. Taxes
You are responsible for any taxes on your sales. Where law requires, we may collect tax information and
report or withhold.

## 8. Prohibited conduct
No fee circumvention, off-platform payment solicitation, fake reviews, shill bidding, manipulating
search, or harassing buyers. No collecting buyer personal data beyond what a sale requires.

## 9. Enforcement
We may remove listings, cancel orders, withhold or reverse points, and suspend or terminate selling
privileges for violations, suspected fraud, or legal/safety reasons.

## 10. Changes
We may update these Seller Terms; the effective date will change and continued selling constitutes
acceptance.
