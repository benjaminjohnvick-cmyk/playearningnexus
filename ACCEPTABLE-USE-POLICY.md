# Acceptable Use Policy — Get Goods Gratis (Free)

> **⚠️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is an inventor-prepared **template** Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for
> qualified counsel to review, tailor, and approve. It unifies, in one place, the prohibited-conduct and
> content rules that currently live per-feature (moderation, Buddy Chat, DMCA), and states how they are
> enforced. Bracketed items **[LIKE THIS]** are for counsel/owner. It supplements — and is incorporated into —
> the Terms of Service. Prepared 2026-09-13.

---

## 0. Scope

This AUP applies to **everyone** who uses the platform (the website/PWA, the mobile apps, and any connected
features such as chat, voice, profiles, reviews, and the optional shopping helper) and to any content you submit
or transmit. All users are **18+**. Breaking this AUP can lead to the actions in §4.

## 1. Prohibited conduct

You will not:

- **Defraud the earn/reward system** — create multiple or fake accounts, use bots/scripts/emulators, farm or
  automate surveys/ads/activities, collude, or otherwise obtain Site Cash, rewards, or prizes you did not
  genuinely earn;
- **Manipulate the closed-loop economy** — attempt to cash out, launder, transfer, sell, or convert Site Cash
  contrary to the Terms, or exploit bugs to mint or move value;
- **Circumvent controls** — bypass security, rate limits, usage caps, age gating, jurisdiction gating, or access
  controls; probe or attack the platform; interfere with its integrity or availability;
- **Scrape or bulk-extract** data, or use the platform to build a competing product;
- **Break the law** — use the platform for anything illegal, or to facilitate illegal goods/services.

## 2. Prohibited content (including user-generated content)

You will not submit, post, or transmit content that:

- is **illegal**, or **child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** — which is subject to **zero tolerance**, removal,
  preservation, and reporting to **NCMEC** and law enforcement as required;
- **harasses, threatens, bullies, or incites violence**, or targets a person or protected group with hate;
- is **sexually explicit, obscene**, or otherwise violates content standards **[counsel/owner to set the exact
  content lines, especially for chat/voice and profiles]**;
- **infringes** intellectual property or privacy rights (see the DMCA/takedown process in `AI-MODERATION-AND-DMCA.md`);
- is **spam, deceptive, impersonating**, or a scam;
- discloses another person's **private or personal information** without authorization.

## 3. Feature-specific rules

- **Buddy Chat / voice** — recorded and transcribed for moderation; no arranging real-world meetups where
  prohibited; Leave/Report/Block must always work. See `BUDDY-CHAT-LEGAL-BRIEF.md`.
- **Profiles** — only the opt-in, limited fields you choose to make public may be shown; no misuse of others'
  profile data.
- **Shopping helper** — use only as intended; no manipulation of cashback/commission.
- **Advertisers/partners** — additionally bound by the Advertiser Agreement / Partner Agreement.

## 4. Enforcement

The Company may, proportionate to the conduct and **[with/without prior notice — counsel to set]**: issue a
warning; remove or restrict content; **suspend or terminate** accounts; **forfeit or reverse** Site Cash,
rewards, or prizes obtained in violation; withhold payouts; and **refer to law enforcement** where appropriate.
Enforcement decisions may combine automated detection with human review. **[Counsel: proportionality, notice,
and the standard for permanent bans.]**

## 5. Reporting and appeals

Users can **report** violations in-app; rights-holders use the DMCA/takedown process. A user affected by an
enforcement action may **[appeal via the stated process — counsel to define the appeal path and timelines]**.

**Advertiser Agreement**, the **Partner Agreement**, and the moderation/DMCA process. If terms conflict,
