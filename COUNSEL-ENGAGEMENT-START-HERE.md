## 1. What this is (30 seconds)

**Get Goods Gratis (Free)** is an 18+ **retail / e-commerce marketplace** (website/PWA + iOS/Android apps) with a
**closed-loop economy**: everyday users earn only non-cashable store credit ("Site Cash"); **real money flows only
between the platform and businesses** (advertisers pay in; affiliates/developers/creators are paid out and
1099-reported). An AI layer generates/optimizes advertising and automates routine ops, with **permanent human
gates on money, identity, and legal actions**. Full plain-language overview: `WEBSITE-SUMMARY-AND-DESCRIPTION.md`.

## 3. Open decisions register (sign-off needed)

### A. Blockers — decide first; most other docs depend on these
1. **Legal entity / "Licensor" identity + type + state of formation.** Referenced as `[LICENSOR]` in the EULA,
   ToS, both Agreements, and the patent assignment. **Form the entity before signing anything** (see §5).
2. **Governing law + arbitration vs. courts + class-action waiver** — must be **identical** across ToS, EULA,
   Partner Agreement, and Advertiser Agreement.
3. **Document precedence order** — app-store license terms > EULA (app) > ToS (service) > publisher EULA
   (third-party product); where the AUP fits.
4. **Closed-loop / money-transmitter posture** — confirm non-cashable Site Cash + business-only cash-out keeps
   the platform outside money-transmission / e-money / stored-value licensing (per launch market).
5. **Prize competition** — confirm the skill-based + AMOE-free-entry structure avoids a lottery/gambling
   characterization; state registration/bonding thresholds; jurisdiction gating. (Highest-variance item.)

### B. Tax (with a CPA / international-tax advisor) — see `Tax & Reporting\`
6. **Marketplace-facilitator characterization** (facilitator vs. reseller/conduit vs. retailer of record) — drives sales-tax collection.
7. **Sales-tax nexus + registration + a tax-calc engine**, and a **product-taxability map** (goods vs. software vs. games) by state.
8. **VAT/GST scope** — which non-US markets; whether to geo-restrict digital sales at launch; EU OSS/IOSS.
9. **1099 items** — filing provider, state filing/TIN-matching, 1099-K interplay per payout rail, TIN security at scale.
10. **Entity income-tax filings + revenue recognition** on prepaid advertising + **tax-reserve** cadence.

### C. Privacy / data — see `Data Collection\`
11. **Lawful basis per data category and market**; whether the **founding "collect everything" first-party**
    posture is adequately disclosed/consented.
12. **Sensitive data** — TIN security at scale; **voice recording** (two-party/wiretap consent + BIPA); **DOB /
    age-assurance** beyond self-attestation given the social/voice feature.
13. **DPIA** (profiling + AI + session screenshots); formalize a **RoPA**; **sub-processor DPAs**; **international
    transfer mechanism** (SCCs); **DSAR** response SLAs; **retention schedule** by category; "**do not sell/share**"
    adequacy; **breach-notification** plan.

### D. Contracts — see `Agreements\`
14. **Partner Agreement** — independent-contractor classification; clawback window; liability cap + indemnity;
    confirm **single-tier referral / no-MLM**; FTC endorsement-disclosure wording; developer **IP ownership**
    (work-for-hire vs. license) + open-source.
15. **Advertiser Agreement** — confirm **prepaid 52-week / 13-cycle, no auto-renew**; SCA/3-D Secure; the 30-day
    proportional-cancellation math (credit, not cash); Full-Value-Guarantee wording (value delivered, **never
    ROI**); right-to-reject + prohibited categories; allocation of advertiser-claim liability.

### E. Policies + IP + remaining
16. **AUP** — exact content standards (chat/voice/profiles); **CSAM/NCMEC** procedure; enforcement notice/appeals.
17. **Accessibility** — target standard (WCAG 2.1 AA) + which regimes (ADA/state/EAA); statement wording; audit
    scope + feedback SLA; vendor flow-down.
18. **IP filings** — provisional → **PCT** timing (within the priority year); **Madrid** trademark designations;
    trademark clearance.
19. **DMCA designated-agent registration** (Copyright Office) so the safe harbor attaches.
20. **Global** — lead-firm international-network capacity; per-market triage (clear / local-review / geo-restrict);
    EU/UK Article 27 representatives.

## 5. Entity flag (do this first)

Nearly every contract and the patent assignment reference a **legal entity that does not formally exist yet**.
Forming it (LLC vs. S-corp vs. C-corp) is the foundation — decide it **with the attorney and CPA together**,
since it drives both liability and tax, then flow the chosen name into every `[LICENSOR]` blank.

## 6. NDA + insurance flags

- **NDA when interviewing firms.** A **retained** attorney owes you confidentiality automatically; a firm you are
  only **interviewing** does not. Before sharing the trade-secret-sensitive patent material
  (`Provisional Patent\PATENT-FEATURE-AND-FUNCTION-DISCLOSURE-BRIEF`, and the "secret-sauce" specifics flagged in
  the provisional's §13), either get a short **mutual NDA** or withhold the sensitive specifics until you retain
  the firm.
- **Insurance.** Ask the attorney which coverages fit this model — **cyber / data-breach, technology E&O,
  media/advertising liability, and general liability** are the usual candidates for a data-collecting,
  ad-driven marketplace.
