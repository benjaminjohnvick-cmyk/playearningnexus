1. PURPOSE OF THIS PACKET
-------------------------
GamerGain is preparing to launch a play-to-earn web and mobile platform. This packet summarizes how the
product works today and flags the areas where we want your review and sign-off before launch. We have
designed the product conservatively and enforced the main risk controls in code (not just policy);
where a control is a legal judgment rather than an engineering one, we have left it OFF by default and
called it out below.

2. WHAT THE PRODUCT IS (PLAIN LANGUAGE)
---------------------------------------
Members complete surveys, offers, and activities and earn "points." Points are a closed-loop, on-
platform unit: within a given market, one point equals one cent of that market's local currency for the
purpose of pricing items inside the app. Points are spent inside the platform — in a store and a
Facebook-Marketplace-style marketplace. Real-money withdrawal ("cash-out") is NOT enabled. Card
charging is OFF by default. The platform uses AI to generate an original product catalog, localize the
experience by country, personalize recommendations, and improve itself under human oversight.

3. THE MONEY MODEL — WHY WE BELIEVE IT IS LOWER-RISK, AND WHAT TO CONFIRM
------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Closed loop. Points are earned and spent inside the platform and are not cashable. In its default
    posture the platform does not transmit money to users. This is enforced in code: cash-out is a
    gated feature, off by default.
(b) Not a stored-value / money-transmission instrument (our position). Because points cannot be
    withdrawn for cash and are a revocable license to use a feature, we take the position that the
    default product is not money transmission or stored value. WE NEED YOUR CONFIRMATION on this,
    per state (US) and per country, especially if/when cash-out is enabled.
(c) Local-currency pricing. Amounts shown to users are converted to their local currency; points track
    local cents.
(d) Card payments. When enabled, card charges run through a third-party processor and a disclosed
    markup applies. A card order is not fulfilled and funds are not released until payment is captured.
    PLEASE ADVISE on processor onboarding, surcharge/markup disclosure rules, and any licensing.
(e) Guarded settings. Minimum age, tax thresholds, caps, points cashability, and affiliate structure
    sit behind hard code bounds and are excluded from the AI optimizer.

QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL (Money):
  - Does the closed-loop points model avoid money-transmitter licensing in our launch states/countries?
  - What triggers, if any, would convert points into a regulated instrument?
  - Requirements before enabling cash-out (licensing, KYC, escheatment/unclaimed-property, tax)?

4. PROMOTIONAL VALUE & ADVERTISING CLAIMS (FTC — PLEASE REVIEW CLOSELY)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
We advertise, on the landing page, in the welcome email, and in-app: "up to $2,000 in first-year value,"
which includes "up to $1,460 in welcome rewards." Key facts:
  - Welcome rewards are NON-CASHABLE promotional credit that covers up to a set share per order
    (default 20%), expire 12 months after signup, and apply to platform-catalog/store items only.
  - The $2,000 figure is the sum of a substantiation table (welcome rewards + premium features + a daily
    "free app time" perk + retailer cash-back + contest entries + referral bonuses). Each line is a
    real, accessible benefit.
  - Every surface shows the figure with "up to" and a disclosure that actual value depends on activity
    and that welcome rewards are non-cashable and expire.
  - We DO NOT make guaranteed-earnings claims; an "earnings projections" UI flag is OFF by default to
    avoid FTC earnings-claim exposure. Marketing says "earn $3+/day" as a general statement — PLEASE
    ADVISE whether this needs an earnings-claim disclaimer / typical-results substantiation.

QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL (Advertising):
  - Is the "up to $X value" presentation + disclosure sufficient under FTC guidance on "free"/"value"?
  - Does "earn $3+/day" require a typical-results disclosure and substantiation file?
  - Any state-specific advertising rules for money-earning apps?

5. THE MARKETPLACE
------------------
Three listing types coexist:
  - Platform catalog: ORIGINAL products offered by GamerGain, fulfilled by our AI-managed order flow.
  - Member listings: users list/resell their own items (relisting shows NO personal information);
    the member seller ships; funds/points release after delivery via an AI-managed escrow flow.
  - Affiliate listings & "find the real thing" search: outbound links to real products sold and
    fulfilled by third-party retailers (Amazon, Google Shopping, eBay, each country's top retailer).
    Where a link is an affiliate link, we disclose it and may earn a commission.
Prohibited/regulated items (weapons, drugs, counterfeit, etc.) are blocked from listings. Buyers pay
with points or card (+markup) or, for real shippable goods, finance with Affirm (Section 7).

IMPORTANT IP POSITION: We DO NOT scrape or copy any retailer's catalog, images, or descriptions. The
platform catalog is original (original titles, descriptions, and AI-generated images). Real branded
goods enter only through a retailer's OFFICIAL affiliate API/links using our authorized credentials.

QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL (Marketplace):
  - Marketplace operator liability, buyer/seller dispute handling, and prohibited-item obligations.
  - Affiliate disclosure adequacy; any marketplace facilitator sales-tax obligations.
  - Confirmation that the original-catalog + authorized-affiliate approach avoids IP/deceptive-ad risk.

6. AI, DATA COLLECTION & PRIVACY
--------------------------------
The platform collects behavioral/interaction data (clicks, navigation, survey-answer timing used for
quality/fraud), account and transaction data, and approximate location from IP (to set country,
currency, language, and flag). AI is used to generate catalog content and images, personalize, power an
assistant, A/B-test proposed changes before release, and optimize non-sensitive settings; material
money/compliance decisions remain under human oversight. Users can OPT OUT of behavioral recording,
DOWNLOAD their data, and DELETE their account (which anonymizes the profile and erases behavioral data,
subject to legally required retention). A subprocessor list is included. Cookie/tracking notice included.

QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL (Privacy):
  - GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA-CPRA and other regional compliance of the Privacy Policy and practices.
  - Whether any automated processing requires additional notice/rights handling.
  - Data-transfer mechanism, DPA templates with subprocessors, and any DPO/representative requirement.
  - EU/UK cookie-consent flow adequacy for the geo-IP and behavioral analytics.

7. AFFIRM BUY-NOW-PAY-LATER (REAL GOODS ONLY)
---------------------------------------------
For real, shippable products only, users may choose Affirm at checkout. Affirm underwrites each buyer
and owns default risk; the platform is paid upfront. Code hard-blocks financing of points, store
credit, gift cards, or any "play" value. Off by default until Affirm merchant approval + keys are set.

QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL (BNPL):
  - Any merchant-side disclosures/obligations when offering third-party BNPL.
  - Confirmation that restricting BNPL to real goods (never points) is the correct boundary.

8. DAILY BOOST (ADVERTISER-FUNDED PERK)
---------------------------------------
"Earn $4 in offers today → 5 minutes of free app time (in-app purchases covered up to a cap)." Funded by
the advertiser revenue from those offers (designed net-neutral). Applies within our own ecosystem, not
third-party app-store purchases.

QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL: any promotional/consideration issues; interaction with platform (Apple/Google)
in-app-purchase rules.

9. AGE, MINORS, SWEEPSTAKES, REFERRALS, TAX
-------------------------------------------
  - Age: 18+ hard floor enforced in code; not directed to children; no knowing collection under 18.
  - Sweepstakes/contests/jackpots: exist as features; any prize promotion is subject to its own posted
    rules and to registration/bonding where applicable. PLEASE ADVISE per jurisdiction; a free-entry
    (AMOE) path exists.
  - Referrals: single-tier by default (multi-level referrals OFF) to avoid pyramid/chain-referral risk.
    PLEASE CONFIRM the referral mechanics and any FTC/state MLM exposure.
  - Tax: reporting thresholds and any backup-withholding rate are configured in guarded settings and
    become relevant if cash-out is enabled. PLEASE ADVISE on 1099/W-9 collection and thresholds.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY / DMCA
--------------------------------
Original platform content; notice-and-takedown + counter-notice handling supported; a designated agent
contact must be set. PLEASE ADVISE on DMCA agent registration and marketplace IP-complaint process.

11. WHAT IS OFF BY DEFAULT (COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS — NOT COST DECISIONS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Cash-out; card charging; multi-level referrals; SMS marketing; store-credit purchase; peer-to-peer
transfers; guaranteed-earnings UI. Each is a single flag that stays OFF until the corresponding legal/
operational prerequisite is met. Behavioral-recording opt-out, data export, and account deletion are
available to every user. Age 18+, prohibited-item blocking, and disclosed affiliate links are always on.
