> `SOCIAL-ENDORSER-AND-REFERRAL-LEGAL-BRIEF`, `SOCIAL-POSTING-ONE-TAP-AND-CONSENT`.

## Why this matters

The paid-endorser program pays members a share of the measured conversion value their `#ad`-disclosed posts
drive. Once a member is compensated to promote offers, the FTC treats them as an endorser with a **material
connection** that must be **clearly and conspicuously disclosed**, and the company can share liability for
deceptive or undisclosed posts. A written agreement sets the rules and allocates that risk.

## What counsel should produce

- **Endorser agreement** — eligibility (18+, opted in), the compensation basis (non-cashable Site Cash share,
  caps), **mandatory `#ad`/#sponsored disclosure** that is enforced and unremovable, **no income claims / no
  unapproved claims** (endorser may use only the advertiser's approved copy), FTC-compliant conduct rules,
  monitoring + takedown rights, clawback for undisclosed/self/fraudulent conversions, term/termination, and
  1099/tax acknowledgment.
- **Disclosure & training copy** the endorser must follow.

## Decisions for counsel

Whether scheduled auto-posting to a consenting member's own account is acceptable or a permanent human "tap Post"
must remain; independent-contractor posture and 1099 treatment of the Site Cash reward; platform liability
allocation for an endorser's non-compliant post; interaction with each social network's own endorsement rules.
Program ships **OFF** (`ENDORSER_ENABLED=0`) until cleared.
