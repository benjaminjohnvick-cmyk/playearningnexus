## The framing: "global patents" is a ladder, not one purchase

Cost stays low until you choose to enter specific countries. The **PCT** preserves worldwide priority for ~2.5
years so the large national-phase spend is **deferred** to month 30 and incurred **only** in the markets you
deliberately pick. Launching US-first (and geo-restricting the app) keeps near-term IP cost modest while global
rights are still secured.

## Phase 1 — Now (US-first + preserve global rights): ~$15,000–$40,000

| Item | Ballpark (USD) |
|---|---|
| US **provisional** (draft already prepared) | gov't ~$130–$320 (micro/small entity) + ~$1,000–$3,000 attorney |
| US **non-provisional / utility** filing | ~$6,000–$15,000 attorney (boutique) + ~$1,820 gov't |
| US **office-action responses** (expect 2–3) | ~$2,000–$6,000 each |
| **PCT** international application (holds ~150+ countries) | ~$4,000 gov't + $2,000–$8,000 attorney = **~$5,500–$12,000** |
| US **trademark** (Madrid base application) | WIPO base ~$720–$995 + ~$1,000–$3,000 attorney |

**What Phase 1 buys:** US patent + trademark protection **and** a locked worldwide patent priority date via the
PCT — for well under ~$40k — with no obligation to spend in any foreign country yet.

## Phase 2 — ~Month 30 (national phase, only in chosen markets): the big variable

Per-country patent entry ~**$5,000–$12,000 each** (government + local agent + translation); translations alone
$3,000–$8,000 per language. Rough per market: **Europe (EPO)** ~$3,000–$7,000 to enter (then per-country
validation on top); **China** ~$2,000–$5,000; **Japan** ~$3,000–$6,000; **Canada** ~$1,500–$3,500; **South
Korea / Australia** ~$2,000–$4,000 each.

**Published lifetime totals (entry + prosecution + maintenance):**

| Scope | Lifetime total (USD) |
|---|---|
| ~3 markets (US + Europe + China) | **~$40,000–$70,000** |
| ~5 markets (US + EPO + China + Japan + Canada) | **~$75,000–$150,000** |
| "Truly everywhere" (30+ countries) | **~$250,000–$500,000+** — why almost no one does this |

## Trademark (Madrid) — much cheaper than patents

WIPO base ~$720–$995 + ~$110–$400+ per country designation. **5–10 countries ≈ $2,500–$5,000 in government
fees**, plus attorney time → **~$5,000–$12,000 all-in** for a solid international footprint. **Copyright** is
near-free (~$65 US registration; automatic in ~180 Berne countries). **Trade secrets** cost nothing to hold
(NDAs + access controls).

## Ongoing (don't forget)

**Patent annuities/maintenance** are paid **per country, per year, and rise over time** — across many countries
they compound into tens of thousands per year at scale. **Trademark renewals** every ~10 years. These are the
hidden driver behind the "truly everywhere" total.

## Bottom line

Near-term (US-first) IP spend is **~$15,000–$40,000** and secures both US protection and worldwide priority. The
large numbers (**$75,000–$150,000+**) arrive only at month 30, only if you expand, and only for the countries you
choose. The PCT is the instrument that lets you defer that decision.

## Assumptions & caveats

- Micro/small-entity USPTO rates assumed where applicable; large-entity fees are higher.
- Software/e-commerce subject matter may need extra office-action responses (higher prosecution cost).
- Ranges assume flat-fee/boutique counsel at the low end, BigLaw at the high end.
- Excludes trademark clearance searches, patent landscape/freedom-to-operate opinions, and litigation/enforcement.
- Foreign figures include typical translation + local-agent costs but vary by country and claim length.
- Deadlines are hard (provisional +12 mo → PCT; TM +6 mo → Paris; priority +30 mo → national phase).
