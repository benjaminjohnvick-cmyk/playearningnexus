# Insurance Coverage — Counsel & Broker Brief

> **⚠️ NOT LEGAL ADVICE / NOT INSURANCE ADVICE.** An inventor-prepared brief listing the coverages to bind before
> launch and why each maps to this platform's risk profile. Work this with a commercial insurance broker;
> counsel confirms contractual insurance requirements. Prepared 2026-09-14. See `LAWYER-MASTER-TODO` §0.

## Why this matters

The platform collects sensitive data (TINs, voice, behavioral), runs advertising with performance-adjacent
claims, hosts user content, and moves real money to partners. Each of those is an insurable risk, and several
counterparty contracts (advertisers, partners, app stores, processors) may require the company to carry and
evidence coverage.

## Coverages to evaluate and bind

- **Cyber / data-breach** — first-party breach response, notification costs, and third-party liability for a
  data incident (ties to the breach-notification plan in the security brief).
- **Technology errors & omissions (Tech E&O)** — failure/defect of the software service.
- **Media & advertising liability** — advertising content, IP/defamation, and endorsement/claims exposure
  (ties to the FTC and endorser items).
- **Commercial general liability (CGL).**
- **Directors & officers (D&O)** — once there is a board/investors.
- **Employment practices (EPLI)** — if/when employees are hired.

## Decisions for counsel/owner

Limits and retentions per coverage; which contracts require the company to carry insurance and at what limits
(and whether to require it of partners/developers in reverse); additional-insured and waiver-of-subrogation
requests; how the closed-loop money model and 18+ gate affect underwriting.
