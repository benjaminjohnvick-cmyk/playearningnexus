## 0. Orientation

This Agreement governs the purchase and delivery of **advertising** on the platform by a business ("Advertiser,"
"you") from **[LICENSOR — legal entity/owner name]** ("Company," "we"). The consumer economy is closed-loop and
governed separately; this document is B2B.

## 1. Acceptance and eligibility

By purchasing or running advertising, you agree to these terms. You represent you are a **business** acting
through an authorized representative, are **18+**, and will comply with law and our policies.

## 2. Advertising products

The Company offers **tiered advertiser products (Tier 1 entry, Tier 2 "Scale," Tier 3 "Unlimited")**, sponsored
listings, premium boosts, and PPC-network advertising (a shared feature across all three tiers), plus a
**founding pre-revenue offer**. Specific inventory, capabilities, targeting, and pricing are described at
purchase and in the monetization documents. **[Counsel: confirm the product descriptions and any capability vs.
impression-volume distinctions are accurately disclosed.]**

## 3. Billing and prepayment

- Each tier is **prepaid for the full 52 weeks up front in a single charge**, recognized across **13 four-week
  cycles**. **No recurring auto-charge / no auto-renew** is built, so there is no negative-option/auto-renew
  exposure on this path. Billing is disclosed as "billed in 13 four-week cycles," never "monthly."
- Card payments use **Strong Customer Authentication (3-D Secure)** where required; a placement activates only
  after payment clears. **[Counsel: confirm SCA + receipt/disclosure language.]**
- You are responsible for applicable **taxes** on your purchase. **[CPA/counsel: sales-tax/VAT treatment of
  advertising services per jurisdiction — see `TAX-OBLIGATIONS-AND-FILINGS-BRIEF.md`.]**

## 4. Cancellation and refunds

A **30-day proportional cancellation** applies: cancel within 30 days of purchase and recover **one-third** of
what you paid (the Company retains two-thirds); the non-refundable portion is disclosed and consent-logged before
it applies, and any refund is issued as **closed-loop advertising credit**, not cash/card. Outside that window,
the Full-Value Delivery Guarantee (§5) governs. **[Counsel: confirm the cancellation math, disclosure, and
credit-not-cash treatment are enforceable and clearly presented.]**

## 5. Delivery — value delivered, results never guaranteed

The Company commits to **deliver advertising value** (impressions/placements measured at a conventional CPM) and,
where the guarantee is enabled, to keep delivering until the purchased value is fulfilled (**make-good only**;
the refund backstop ships off). The "dollar amount" is **advertising value the Company measures and controls — it
is never your revenue, sales, or ROI, and there is no results or money-back guarantee.** Delivery may extend
beyond the initial term as the audience grows. See `FULL-VALUE-DELIVERY-GUARANTEE.md`. **[Counsel: confirm this
is a clean service-level commitment with no performance-guarantee/credit character; finalize the disclosure
wording.]**

## 6. Reporting and metrics

The Company provides measured performance metrics (e.g., impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, ROAS,
and network metrics such as eCPM/IPM/CPP and windowed ROAS) computed from **real activity** and benchmarked
against standard norms. **Every figure is measured, shown with its basis, and never a guarantee of ROI**; below a
data threshold the Company says "still gathering data" rather than fabricating numbers. Off-platform revenue is
counted only when you report it, stored flagged/unverified. See `ADVERTISER-AI-REPORTS.md`.

## 7. Creative — content standards and licensing

- You grant the Company a license to host, display, and (where you request AI-assisted creative) generate and
  optimize your advertising across owned and, where you opt in, social surfaces.
- **AI-assisted/AI-generated creative** carries a visible "AI-generated" label and C2PA provenance
  (`AI-CONTENT-DISCLOSURE-DESIGN.md`).
- You represent your creative and the products/claims in it are **truthful, non-misleading, lawful, and
  non-infringing**, with substantiation for claims. The Company may **reject, pause, or remove** any advertising
  at its discretion (e.g., prohibited categories, policy or legal risk). **[Counsel: prohibited-category list +
  right-to-reject language; who bears liability for advertiser claims.]**

## 8. Advertiser responsibilities and compliance

You are solely responsible for your products, offers, landing pages, and legal compliance (including your own
advertising, consumer-protection, and privacy obligations). You will not use the platform to advertise unlawful
goods/services or to infringe rights. **[Counsel: FTC advertising-law allocation; indemnity for advertiser
content.]**

## 9. Confidentiality, term, termination

Each party protects the other's non-public information. The Agreement runs for the purchased term; the Company
may suspend/terminate for breach, non-payment, or legal risk; cancellation/refund is governed by §4. Survival:
IP, confidentiality, disclaimers, liability, governing law.

## 10. Disclaimers and limitation of liability

Advertising is provided "as is"; the Company disclaims warranties to the maximum extent permitted by law and
**does not guarantee revenue, ROI, or specific results**. Total liability is limited to **[amounts you paid for
the affected advertising — counsel to set]**; no indirect/consequential damages. **[Counsel: carve-outs.]**
