## Screening questions to ask each firm (pre-filled from the global-launch brief)

1. **International reach:** *Do you have an international network, or can you quarterback local counsel in my
   target markets?* (The decisive question — a "yes" is what makes a single lead firm workable for a global
   launch. Warner Norcross + Judd's TerraLex membership is a concrete yes.)
2. **Closed-loop firewall:** Can you confirm my non-cashable, closed-loop store-credit model keeps me outside
   money-transmitter / e-money licensing per target market, and flag any market where stored-value rules attach?
3. **Prize competition:** Can you assess the skill-based + free-entry (AMOE) prize competition for
   sweepstakes/lottery/gambling exposure per jurisdiction? (Highest-variance item.)
4. **IP filings:** Can you handle the trademark via the **Madrid Protocol** and the patent via the **PCT**
   (provisional already drafted)?
5. **Privacy:** Can you set up GDPR/UK-GDPR compliance, the EU/UK Article 27 representatives, and a lawful
   international-transfer mechanism?
6. **Tax coordination:** Can you coordinate with an international-tax advisor on VAT OSS and
   marketplace-facilitator/nexus? (Tax is a CPA-network job run in parallel.)
7. **Per-market triage:** Will you sort my target-market list into *clear with baseline / needs local review /
   geo-restrict*, and sign off on a phased rollout?
8. **Retail/e-commerce + AI:** Do you have a retail/e-commerce practice, and are you comfortable with
   AI-generated advertising content and AI-content disclosure obligations?

**Suggested structure (per the global brief):** a **Grand Rapids lead firm** — Warner Norcross + Judd or Varnum
are the strongest local fits — as the hub, with a national retail specialist (**Perkins Coie** or **Baker
McKenzie**) engaged for the international promotions/privacy layer, plus an international-tax advisor alongside.
