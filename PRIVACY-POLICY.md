# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 28, 2026** · Version 2026-07-28

This Privacy Policy explains how GamerGain ("GamerGain," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and
protects personal information when you use our website, progressive web app, and mobile applications
(the "Service"). It also describes the choices and rights you have over your information.

> This is a plain-language template describing current practice. It is not legal advice. Have counsel
> confirm it against the privacy laws that apply to you (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other
> regional laws) before you rely on it.

## 1. Who we are
GamerGain operates a play-to-earn platform where users complete activities to earn points and use those
points in a store and marketplace. The data controller is GamerGain; contact details are in Section 12.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account information** you provide: name or display name, email, password (stored only as a secure
  hash), and country or region.
- **Transaction information**: points earned and spent, orders, and, where card payments are enabled,
  limited payment metadata processed by our payment processor (we do not store full card numbers).
- **Activity and behavioral information**: pages viewed, clicks and interactions, survey responses and
  the timing/patterns of those responses (used to assess answer quality and detect fraud), device and
  browser type, approximate location derived from your IP address (used to set your country, currency,
  language, and flag), and app performance data.
- **Communications**: messages you send to support or through in-Service assistants.
- **Third-party activity data**: completion signals from survey and offer providers.

## 3. How we use information
We use information to: operate your account and the points economy; provide the store, marketplace, and
earning activities; localize the Service (currency, language, and country) based on your location;
personalize recommendations and assistant interactions; generate and improve original catalog content;
test proposed changes before releasing them; detect and prevent fraud and abuse; provide support;
comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations; and maintain the security and integrity of the
Service.

## 4. Automated processing and AI
We use automated systems and artificial intelligence to personalize content, power an in-Service
assistant, evaluate survey-answer quality, and optimize non-sensitive settings. These systems inform
how the Service is presented to you. Decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, and any
changes to money-related or compliance-related controls, remain subject to human oversight. You can
contact us to ask about automated processing that affects you.

## 5. Behavioral recording and your control over it
To improve the Service, we record in-Service behavioral events (such as clicks and navigation). You can
**opt out of behavioral recording** in your privacy settings; when you opt out, behavioral capture
stops on your next load. Opting out does not stop essential processing needed to run your account and
transactions.

## 6. How we share information
We share information with: **service providers** (hosting, database, payment processing, email/SMS,
analytics, AI/LLM and image-generation providers, and IP-geolocation and exchange-rate providers) that
process data on our behalf under contract; **third-party earning providers** as needed to credit your
activity; **affiliate retailers** only to the extent you follow an outbound affiliate or search link
(we do not send them your account data); and **authorities or others** where required by law, to
enforce our Terms, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service. We do **not** sell
your personal information for money. Where "sharing" for cross-context advertising is regulated, you may
opt out as described in Section 8.

## 7. International transfers
We may process and store information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate
safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for cross-border transfers.

## 8. Your rights and choices
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict your
personal information, to object to certain processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising or certain
sharing. In the Service you can:
- **Download your data** (a machine-readable export that includes your behavioral, AI-profile, and
  marketplace data);
- **Delete your account** (this anonymizes your profile and erases behavioral data, subject to records
  we must retain by law);
- **Opt out of behavioral recording** as described above.
To exercise other rights, contact us using Section 12. We will not discriminate against you for
exercising your rights.

## 9. Data retention
We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service,
then for the period required to meet legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, and fraud-prevention
obligations, after which we delete or anonymize it.

## 10. Security
We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information,
including hashing of passwords and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## 11. Children
The Service is for users **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone
under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it.
