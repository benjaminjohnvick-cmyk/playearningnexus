# Cookie & Tracking Notice

**Effective date: July 28, 2026**

This notice explains the cookies, local storage, and tracking GamerGain (the "Service") uses. It
supplements the Privacy Policy. Not legal advice; in the EU/UK, non-essential storage generally requires
consent — confirm your consent flow with counsel.

## What we store on your device
- **Strictly necessary (no consent required):** authentication token (keeps you signed in), and local
  preferences such as your selected language and currency and cached exchange rates and detected country
  (so the app loads localized without re-fetching). The Service uses in-memory/local storage for these;
  it does not rely on third-party advertising cookies.
- **Functional:** UI state and recently viewed items, to make the app usable.
- **Analytics / product improvement:** in-app behavioral events (clicks, navigation, survey-answer
  timing) used to improve the Service and detect fraud. **You can turn this off** in privacy settings
  (behavioral-recording opt-out); essential functionality continues.

## Third-party calls that involve your device
- **IP geolocation** (to set your country/flag/currency/language) sends your IP to a geolocation
  provider; the result is cached on your device.
- **Exchange rates** are fetched from a rates provider (no personal data).
- **Outbound retailer links** ("find the real thing"): when you tap one you leave the app for a
  third-party site with its own cookies and policies.

## What we do NOT do (in this build)
- No third-party advertising cookies, no cross-app tracking / ad IDs, no selling of personal
  information.

## Your choices
- **Behavioral recording:** opt out anytime in privacy settings.
- **Browser controls:** you can clear local storage/cookies in your browser or app settings (doing so
  signs you out and resets preferences).
- **Consent (EU/UK):** where required, a consent banner should gather consent for non-essential storage
  before it is set, and record the choice. Wire your banner to gate the analytics/behavioral capture.
