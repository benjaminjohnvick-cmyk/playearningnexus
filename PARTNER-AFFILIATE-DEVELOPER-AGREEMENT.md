## 0. Orientation

This Agreement governs a **business relationship** in which a partner is paid **real money** (a revenue share,
commission, or fee) for referring business, supplying integrations/content, or endorsing the platform. It does
**not** apply to everyday consumer users, who are closed-loop (non-cashable Site Cash) and are governed by the
Terms of Service. **[Counsel: confirm one combined agreement vs. separate Affiliate / Developer / Creator
addenda; the core terms below are shared, with role-specific schedules.]**

## 1. Parties and relationship

This Agreement is between **[LICENSOR — legal entity/owner name]** ("Company," "we") and the person or business
accepting it ("Partner," "you"). You are an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, partner, or
joint venturer. You have no authority to bind the Company. Nothing here creates an exclusive relationship.

## 2. Eligibility

You must be **18+** (or the age of majority where you are) and, where you act as a business, authorized to
contract. You must provide accurate identity and, where paid at/over the reporting threshold, **tax
documentation** (§6). **[Counsel: any sanctions/export screening or restricted-jurisdiction list.]**

## 3. The programs (role-specific)

- **Affiliate** — refers businesses/users via approved links/codes; paid a revenue share/commission on qualified,
  verified activity. Referral is **single-tier** (no multi-level/downline compensation).
- **Developer** — supplies integrations, software, or digital products for the store/marketplace; paid a
  revenue share on sales/usage.
- **Creator / Endorser** — supplies content or endorsements; paid per the applicable schedule and subject to the
  FTC disclosure obligations in §8 and `SOCIAL-ENDORSER-AND-REFERRAL-LEGAL-BRIEF.md`.

Specific rates, attribution windows, and qualification rules are set out in the applicable **Schedule / dashboard
terms**, which the Company may update prospectively on notice. **[Counsel: change-of-terms mechanism + notice.]**

## 4. Payment and revenue share

- Payments are the **revenue share/commission/fee** stated in your Schedule, calculated from the Company's
  **server-authoritative money ledger**, paid in real currency via the enabled rails (e.g., PayPal/Venmo/Cash
  App) on the stated cadence, subject to a **minimum payout threshold** and fraud/clawback holds (§7).
- Consumer Site Cash is **not** money and is never paid to partners as cash; partner payouts are ordinary
  vendor payments. **[Counsel: confirm payment terms, currency, FX, and processor-agreement consistency.]**

## 5. Fraud, verification, and clawback

Payments are earned only on **genuine, verified** activity. The Company may withhold, reverse, or **claw back**
amounts tied to fraud, self-dealing, incentivized/undisclosed traffic, chargebacks, returns, duplicate/multi-
accounting, or breach. Referral rewards are activity-tied and clawback-gated. **[Counsel: clawback window +
dispute process.]**

## 6. Taxes (W-9 / 1099 / backup withholding)

You are responsible for your own taxes. Before we can pay you at/over the reporting threshold without
withholding, you must submit a valid **W-9** (or the applicable W-8/foreign equivalent). Absent a W-9 on file at
or above the **$600** 1099-NEC threshold, **24% backup withholding** applies, and we file a **1099-NEC** for
qualifying payments. See `TAX-1099-PIPELINE.md` and `TAX-OBLIGATIONS-AND-FILINGS-BRIEF.md`. **[Counsel/CPA:
confirm thresholds, foreign-payee handling, and TIN security.]**

## 7. Intellectual property

- **Company IP.** We own the platform, marks, and content; you get a limited, revocable license to use approved
  links, codes, and marketing assets solely to perform under this Agreement, per our brand guidelines.
- **Partner-supplied material.** You grant us a license to use what you supply (content, integrations) to
  operate and promote the platform. **[Counsel: for Developers, decide ownership/licensing of integrations and
  any code — work-made-for-hire vs. license — and open-source obligations.]**
- **Feedback** may be used without obligation to you.

## 8. Your representations and compliance

You represent that you will: comply with law and our policies (including the **Acceptable Use Policy**); make
truthful, non-misleading claims and **clearly disclose the material connection** (FTC endorsement guides) in any
endorsement/referral content; not make earnings or results guarantees on our behalf; not spam, cloak, or use
prohibited traffic; not infringe third-party rights; and protect any user data you touch per our Privacy Policy
and applicable law. **[Counsel: endorsement-disclosure wording + monitoring obligation.]**

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other's non-public information and uses it only to perform this Agreement.

## 10. Term, termination, and suspension

Either party may terminate on notice; we may suspend or terminate immediately for breach, fraud, or legal risk.
On termination, licenses end, you stop using our marks/links, and **accrued, verified, non-clawed-back** amounts
are paid on the normal cycle. Sections that should survive (IP, confidentiality, taxes, disclaimers, liability,
governing law) survive.
