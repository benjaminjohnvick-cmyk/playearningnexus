# Social Advertising Disclosure — the one-click "Join" opt-in

*Plain-language disclosure shown at one-click join (version `social-amp-join-1`) and available anytime in
Settings. Recorded to the append-only consent ledger. Not legal advice — counsel should review before launch.*

## What one-click Join does

Tapping **Join with one click** creates your membership and turns **on** social advertising rewards. You see
this disclosure first and can **opt out in the same click** (uncheck the box) or turn it off anytime in
Settings. Opting out never affects your membership.

## What you're agreeing to (and what we can't do)

- **You connect each account yourself — we can't do it silently.** There is no "connect all my accounts"
  switch, because no website can reach into another platform's account. Each platform (Instagram, X, TikTok,
  Facebook, LinkedIn, …) shows you **its own authorization screen**, and you approve the ones you want — one
  tap each. You choose which accounts to connect, and you can disconnect any of them anytime.
- **You always tap "Post."** When an AI-generated ad is ready, it's placed in your composer with the caption
  ready; **you** press Post or Share. Nothing is ever posted to your accounts automatically.
- **Every ad is clearly labeled.** Posts carry an **`#ad`** sponsorship disclosure, as the FTC requires for
  paid promotion.
- **Your reach is counted as advertising value.** Your follower counts on connected accounts are used to
  estimate how many people your posts reach. That estimated reach counts toward the advertiser's **delivered
  advertising value** — it is a measured estimate, never a promised result.
- **Rewards are variable and non-cashable.** Any rewards for participating are variable, are paid only as **Site
  Cash** (closed-loop store credit that spends on-site and is not redeemable for cash), and are **never
  guaranteed**.
- **Your data.** We store only the connection and the follower/reach numbers needed to run the program. We do
  not scan your private messages or post without your tap. You can revoke a connection at any time on the
  platform and in Settings.

## How to opt out

- **At join:** uncheck "include my social accounts for advertising rewards" and choose **Join without social
  ads**.
- **Anytime after:** Settings → Social Advertising → turn off. You can also disconnect any individual account
  on that platform.

## What's recorded

When you join, we record — to an append-only consent ledger — that this disclosure (version `social-amp-join-1`)
was **shown** and **acknowledged**, and whether you **opted in or out**. This is your clickwrap consent; it is
never a silent enrollment.
