# Refund Policy

**Effective date: July 28, 2026** · Version 2026-07-28

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work on GamerGain (the "Service"). It applies together with our
Terms of Service.

> This is a plain-language template describing current practice. It is not legal advice, and consumer
> law in your market may grant rights that override parts of it. Have counsel confirm it before launch.

## 1. Points are a closed-loop unit
Points are an on-platform unit used to price and buy items inside the Service. Within a market, 1 point
equals one cent of the local currency for pricing purposes. Points have no cash value outside the
Service and are generally **non-refundable for cash**. Where we resolve an issue in your favor, we
normally do so by **restoring points** or providing store credit rather than a cash refund.

## 2. Marketplace purchases
- **Member listings** (items shipped by other users): if an item is not delivered, is significantly not
  as described, or the order otherwise fails, contact support within the dispute window shown on your
  order. Our AI-managed fulfillment process holds the transaction until it is resolved; where a claim is
  upheld, points spent are returned to you and the sale is reversed. Sellers are responsible for
  shipping and for the accuracy of their listings.
- **Platform-catalog items**: if a platform order cannot be fulfilled, we refund the points spent (or,
  for card payments, the amount charged) for that order.
- **Affiliate listings and outbound product search**: when you follow a link to a third-party retailer,
  that retailer sells and fulfills the item and sets its own return and refund policy. Those purchases
  are between you and the retailer; our Refund Policy does not apply to them.

## 3. Card payments
Where card payments are enabled, a platform markup may apply and is disclosed before you pay. Approved
refunds of card payments are returned to the original payment method, less any non-refundable
third-party fees where permitted by law. Refund timing depends on your card issuer.

## 4. Digital items, perks, and memberships
Digital goods, perks, entries, and membership fees are generally non-refundable once delivered or once a
benefit period has begun, except where required by law or where the item was not delivered as described.

## 5. Earning reversals and fraud
Points credited in error, through system fault, or through fraud, abuse, duplicate accounts, or
violation of our Terms may be adjusted, withheld, or reversed. This is not a "refund" to you; it is a
correction of an improper credit.

## 6. How to request a refund or open a dispute
Contact support at **[insert support email]** or use the dispute option on the relevant order, within
any window shown. Tell us your account, the order, and what went wrong, and include any evidence
(photos, tracking, screenshots). We aim to acknowledge disputes promptly and resolve them within a
reasonable time.

## 7. Chargebacks
If you believe a card charge is wrong, contact us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a
chargeback. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may result in account suspension pending review.

## 8. Changes
We may update this Policy; we will change the effective date above and, for material changes, take
reasonable steps to notify you.
