# NDA Templates — Counsel Brief

> **⚠️ NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** An inventor-prepared brief requesting two confidentiality templates counsel should
> produce, and describing how they are used across the packet. Prepared 2026-09-14. See `LAWYER-MASTER-TODO`
> §0 and Part B, and the provisional patent's trade-secret note (§13).

## Why this matters

Trade-secret-sensitive material (the provisional patent specifics, the AI-model design, the optimization engine,
pricing internals) loses protection once disclosed without confidentiality in place. Any conversation with a law
firm you have not yet retained, a prospective developer, an advertiser doing deep diligence, or a potential
partner should sit behind an NDA first.

## Templates counsel should produce

- **Mutual NDA** — for two-way discussions (prospective counsel before engagement, potential partners,
  co-marketing counterparties). Covers definition of confidential information, permitted use, exclusions,
  term/survival for trade secrets, return/destruction, and no-license language.
- **One-way (inbound-protective) NDA** — for parties receiving the company's confidential material (contractors,
  beta testers, vendors, service providers), with an IP-assignment/feedback clause where the counterparty may
  create work product.

## Decisions for counsel

Trade-secret survival period (often perpetual for true trade secrets vs. a fixed term for other confidential
info); governing law/venue (align with the rest of the contract stack); whether the one-way NDA should fold into
the Partner/Developer Agreement or stand alone; carve-outs for residual knowledge.

## Where used

Front-of-packet counsel engagement (share patent material only after a mutual NDA); contractor/developer
onboarding; any diligence by advertisers or partners.
